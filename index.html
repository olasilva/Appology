welcome to a little thought…

For the girl whose heart I should have handled more gently ❤️

A small page for a big “I’m sorry”.

My love,

I’ve been thinking deeply about everything I’ve done to you, and honestly, I feel ashamed of the way I’ve made you feel about yourself.

You trusted me with your heart, and instead of being the person who made you feel safe, beautiful, and appreciated, I became someone who made you question your worth.

Involving my sister and allowing you to feel embarrassed, especially at a time when you were already feeling vulnerable, was wrong of me. You never deserved to feel like your sacrifices meant nothing or that you were only in my life for the benefits.

You are so much more to me than what you do for me, and I’m sorry my actions ever made you feel otherwise. I can’t undo the embarrassment or the hurt I’ve caused you, and I won’t insult your feelings by pretending a simple apology can erase everything.

I’m not asking you to forget what happened or heal overnight. I just want you to know that I see where I went wrong, and I genuinely want to become a better man for you. You deserve to feel loved without having to question your worth, and I’m deeply sorry I made you feel otherwise.

I love you, and I know that if I truly mean that, I have to start showing it in the way I treat you, not just in the words I say.

I’m sorry, my love. ❤️

and finally…

You don’t owe me instant forgiveness. I just want to show you, through my actions, that I’m learning to love you better.

made with love (and a little courage) 💕
